The one-scanner household

The problem

In most homes, only one printer ever got its Scan-to-Email configured — usually by whoever set up the router. Everyone else in the house doesn't have their own address on it, so instead of scanning their own paperwork, they hand it to that one person and wait.

How SendScan helps

Every person in the household can register their own address on that exact same physical printer. Photograph it once, and everyone gets their own SendScan address tied to that one device — same printer, separate mailboxes.

Getting set up

Nothing to understand about SMTP, ports, or certificates. Photograph the printer, paste in the config we give you, once, on the printer's touchscreen. Five minutes — the last time anyone in the house needs to ask for help scanning something.

IT's never-ending scanner queue

How SendScan helps

Getting set up

Exactly the same five-minute process as the first time — nothing to remember from before, nothing to migrate by hand.

Frequently asked questions

What is SendScan?

SendScan is a service that generates ready-to-use Scan-to-Email (SMTP) settings for your printer from a single photo, so you can set it up once and start scanning straight to your inbox — no IT knowledge required.

How does the setup actually work?

You photograph your printer, our AI identifies the model and produces the exact SMTP configuration and instructions for that device. You enter it once in your printer's Scan-to-Email menu and it works from then on. If we can't confidently identify your exact model, we show you the generic SMTP settings screen so you can enter it manually.

Which printers are supported?

We aim to support any printer, not a fixed list. Our AI is designed to identify the make and model from a photo generally, with a manual fallback for anything it can't recognize confidently.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each printer gets its own dedicated address and mailbox, and your scans are only ever accessible to your account.
