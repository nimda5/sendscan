Frequently asked questions

What is SendScan?

SendScan is a service that generates ready-to-use Scan-to-Email (SMTP) settings for your printer from a single photo, so you can set it up once and start scanning straight to your inbox — no IT knowledge required.

How does the setup actually work?

You photograph your printer, our AI identifies the model and produces the exact SMTP configuration and instructions for that device. You enter it once in your printer's Scan-to-Email menu and it works from then on. If we can't confidently identify your exact model, we show you the generic SMTP settings screen so you can enter it manually.

Which printers are supported?

We aim to support any printer, not a fixed list. Our AI is designed to identify the make and model from a photo generally, with a manual fallback for anything it can't recognize confidently.

Is my data private?

Yes. Each printer gets its own dedicated address and mailbox, and your scans are only ever accessible to your account.

How much does it cost?

SendScan costs €10 per year, per printer or scanner. Anyone who signs up before launch gets their first year free.

When does SendScan launch?

We're in final preparation. Signing up above reserves your free first year and triggers an email the moment SendScan launches.

Is SendScan good for home use, not just offices?

Yes. SendScan works on a single home printer just as well as on an office fleet — each family member can register their own address on the same physical device, so scanning stops being something you ask one person in the house to do for you.

Can an IT admin manage scan-to-email for many printers with SendScan?

Yes. Each printer gets its own photo-based setup and its own address, so an admin can roll out SendScan across a whole fleet of mixed-brand printers without learning a different SMTP menu for each one, and onboard new employees without touching the printer again.

Can a visitor or guest use SendScan on someone else's printer?

Yes. Setting up SendScan only requires physical access to the printer's screen for a few minutes — it doesn't require an administrator to add you to that organization's address book first.

What happens to my SendScan setup if I replace my printer?

You photograph the new printer and receive a fresh configuration for it. Your scan history and address keep working — replacing the hardware doesn't mean starting over.
